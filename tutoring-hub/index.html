Writer's & Reader's Guide

Semicolon ( ; ) joins two complete sentences that are closely related.
Colon ( : ) follows a complete sentence to introduce a list, quote or explanation.
Parallel structure: items in a series share the same grammatical form.
Independent clause = subject + verb + complete thought.
Central idea: what the whole text is mostly about — not one detail.
Theme: the message about life, usually stated as a full sentence, never one word.
Tone: the author's attitude (e.g., nostalgic, critical, hopeful).
RACE for written answers: Restate · Answer · Cite evidence · Explain.
