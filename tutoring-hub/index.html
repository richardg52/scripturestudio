Writer's & Reader's Guide

Semicolon ( ; ) joins two complete sentences.
Colon ( : ) follows a complete sentence to introduce a list or explanation.
Comma + FANBOYS (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so) joins two sentences.
Parallel structure: list items share the same form.
Central idea: what the WHOLE text is mostly about.
Theme: a message about life, written as a full sentence.
Tone: the author's attitude.
RACE: Restate · Answer · Cite evidence · Explain.
STEAL: Speech · Thoughts · Effect on others · Actions · Looks.
