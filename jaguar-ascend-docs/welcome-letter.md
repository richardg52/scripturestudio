Dear Zeke and the Wilson family,

I am excited to introduce **Jaguar Ascend**, a personal tutoring program I designed and built for Zeke this semester. It covers the three subjects that matter most in 9th grade at DeSoto Central: **Algebra I, English I, and Reading**.

I built it because I believe Zeke can succeed, and a rough start does not define a student. Every student deserves patient, step-by-step teaching that meets him where he is. Jaguar Ascend is my way of making sure Zeke has that every day, at home and on his own schedule.

## What Jaguar Ascend is

Jaguar Ascend works like a private tutor that never runs out of patience. Its built-in guide, **Coach J**, teaches every new skill the way a great teacher does: explain it, show a complete worked example step by step, then let Zeke try one himself.

- **Placement first.** Three short check-ins (not graded) show exactly what Zeke already knows, so lessons start in the right place.
- **45 lessons over 18 weeks**, aligned to the Mississippi College & Career Readiness Standards that DeSoto Central teaches.
- **Help when he misses.** If Zeke gets a problem wrong, Coach J walks through *his* problem, then gives him a similar one to try right away.
- **Practice that never repeats.** Algebra problems are generated fresh every time, alongside 400+ English questions and 18 reading passages.
- **Real measurement.** Quizzes, exit tickets, a post-test, and **The Ascend**, a combined exam in Weeks 1, 9, and 18 that shows his growth.
- **A live report card** with grades, strengths, and the exact skills to work on next.

## The work it takes

Progress comes from steady, daily effort: about **1 hour 45 minutes, Monday through Thursday**, plus a short quiz on Fridays. That is roughly 7 hours a week for 18 weeks.

| Day | What Zeke does | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | New lesson with Coach J: learn and practice together | about 1 hr 45 min |
| Tuesday | Finish the lesson and take the 5-question exit ticket | about 1 hr 45 min |
| Wednesday | Skill practice on the week's lessons | about 1 hr 45 min |
| Thursday | Smart Review of his weakest skills | about 1 hr 45 min |
| Friday | Unit quiz, or review | 15–25 min |

Each day splits into **Algebra I (45 min), English I (30 min), and Reading (30 min)**. The dashboard's "Today's mission" lists exactly what to do, so there is never any guessing.

**How you can help as parents:**

- Set a regular time and a quiet place, and keep the phone on Do Not Disturb.
- Ask Zeke to *teach you* one thing he learned. If he can explain it, he owns it.
- Look at the Report Card together every Friday and celebrate the gains, not just the grades.
- Read his "Show me" answers in the Parent Portal and add a word of encouragement.
